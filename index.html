01 · Our Heritage

A sanctuary built around a courtyard

Like the nalukettu houses of old Kerala, our hospital gathers every room around an open nadumuttam, so that light, rain and the scent of the herb garden are part of every treatment. Our physicians follow the classical texts, and our therapists train for years in the Kerala tradition.

Vata

Air and ether. Movement, nerves, joints. Calmed by warmth, oil and routine.

Pitta

Fire and water. Digestion, skin, intellect. Cooled by bitter herbs and rest.

Kapha

Earth and water. Structure, immunity, calm. Lightened by movement and spice.

Health is not the absence of illness, but a balance we tend, season by season.
